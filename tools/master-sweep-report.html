What the Master dial does to each scene's picture. Every scene was rendered on the same synthetic track at each Master setting, and the finished frames were measured the same way the Master card measures them live. 100 is about as far as scenes go on each measure; the few that go further show past it here and pin at 100 on the card.

Every scene at a glance

Each cell shows the reading at 1.00×. The bar under it spans the lowest to highest reading anywhere on the dial, and the white tick marks where 1.00× sits. The last column names the measure the dial moves most and how. A spread under about 10, or under a sixth of the reading itself, is run-to-run noise, so a scene whose every measure stays inside that barely changes with the dial.
