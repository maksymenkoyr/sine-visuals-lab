fix-conflicts: in a terminal, or `! fix-conflicts` in a session (function in ~/.zshrc; also npm run fix-conflicts) — a cheap headless model resolves a stopped rebase/merge's conflicts (tools/fix-conflicts.sh); `fix-conflicts <PR number>` first finds or creates that PR's worktree under .claude/worktrees/, moves into it and starts `git rebase origin/<base>` there unless one is already stopped (terminal only)
