Measured on the reference
  • The 3-hour video is one 60:00 loop played three times; frames at t and t+3600 s match (r 1.00).
  • No shot cuts: the camera keeps flying in, about ×1.08 closer per second, and half the picture is new every ~4 s.
  • The colours turn round the hue wheel about once every 34 s.
  • Mirrored left to right for 45% of the hour, in three long runs; new places arrive by ~1.5 s dissolves.
  • It does not follow its music: how fast the picture changes tracks loudness at r ≤ 0.18 at every lag (chance alone reaches 0.13), and 0 of 19 picture changes land on a beat with a hit.
How our scene could react (proposal)
  • Loudness sets the flight speed: quiet passages drift, loud ones push in.
  • Kicks and claps flash the cell edges for a moment.
  • A new song section dissolves to a new place.
  • In silence the camera hovers and the colours slow almost to a stop.
