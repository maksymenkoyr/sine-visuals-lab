Readable Pairs pads

You raised three problems with the Pairs pads: the live monitors barely change, the − / + axes don't say what a value does, and dragging a pad often changes nothing you can see in the scene. Before proposing fixes I ran the pads' own culture code across the whole range of values. The numbers explain all three problems. The lab below has every idea as a switch, so you can judge them by dragging.

What the numbers say

1

Two thirds of each Smell axis does nothing.

Overlap, the share of ground two strains hold together, stays flat from −1.5 to 0 and again from +1 to +1.5. Only 0 to +1 changes the picture. Avoiding needs no strength: each strain already sticks to its own trail, so the two stay apart whether A1 avoids B2 a little or a lot.

2

The default table sits in the flat part.

Every cross value in the default Smell table is below zero. Starting from the default look, a drag changes nothing until it crosses the zero line. That is the "often we don't see any change" moment.

3

The monitor hides the part that does change.

Between 0 and +1 the trails change steadily, but the pad clips bright trails to full brightness and adds the two strain colours together. A1 red plus B2 amber comes out as one orange fog, so you can't tell who went where.

4

Eat only bites where the strains meet.

Under the default Smell, A1 eating B2 removes at most 6% of B2's trail, because they never share ground. Make them attract each other (+0.6 both ways) and the same Eat removes up to 58%. Feed works either way, but gently: up to +22%.

5

Speed is fine.

A pad settles on a new value in 60 to 120 steps, 1 to 2 seconds at the pads' rate. The problem is where the change happens, not how fast.

Smell: overlap as one direction moves
Touch: B2's trail as A1's steps eat or feed it

Measured on the pads' own culture code (physarum2Preview.ts) with the shipped strain motion: 500 steps, 4 seeds per point, no beat reseeds, the other direction left at its default. Overlap counts 6×6-cell blocks, so separate territories score low and crossing networks high. Script: docs/scenes/physarum2/scripts/padresponse.mjs.

The ideas

Grouped by the three problems you named. Every chip here mirrors the one in the lab.

Problem 1

The monitor barely shows any change

M1Recommended

Contact colours

Each strain keeps its own colour, and ground they share turns white. Separate territories, crossing paths and full merging become three different pictures instead of three shades of the same mix.

Fixes finding 3. Only the pad's pixel pass changes, which is cheap.

M2Recommended

Own exposure

Scale each strain to its own brightest trail, re-measured every third of a second, instead of one fixed exposure. Dense paths stop clipping to white and a thin strain doesn't vanish next to a thick one.

Fixes the clipping half of finding 3.

M3Recommended

Overlap meter

A thin strip along the bottom of each pad with the pair's overlap and its last 10 seconds as a trace. When a drag does nothing, you see a flat line. When it works, the line climbs before your eye picks up the change.

The same number as the charts above, from a pass over 144 blocks four times a second per pad.

M4Recommended

Settle on release

When you let go, the pad runs 120 steps at once, about 2 seconds of growth in roughly 15 ms of work, and shows where the new value lands. A short glow marks the jump.

Turns "did it change?" into an instant before/after.

M5Recommended

Hold still while touched

The pad under your pointer skips beat reseeds, so the change you made isn't scrambled while you look at it. The other pads and the scene keep their beats.

Turn Beat reseeds off in the dish to see how much they hide.

M6Your call

Crisp pixels

Draw the 72-cell culture as hard pixels instead of smoothing it up to the pad's size. Sharper and more like a lab readout. You may find it blocky.

Today's smoothing is part of the fog in your screenshot.

Problem 2

The axes don't say what a value does

W1Recommended

Words at the axis ends

Replace − and + with what the value does, read from the first strain: avoids … seeks for Smell, eats … feeds for Touch. The middle caption stays A1 → B2. Pick the words in the lab.

Watch the direction with "repels · attracts". A1 → B2 at +1 means A1 follows B2's trail, so B2 attracts A1, not the reverse. With those words the hint flips the sentence to keep it true. Seeks and avoids are the plain words for chemotaxis, the slime mould's own steering.

W2Recommended

Hint at the cursor

A small card beside the pointer while you hover or drag: both directions as plain sentences with their numbers, each strain in its colour. It adds a line when you're in a flat stretch, or when a Touch pair never meets.

Verbs only. No outcome names like Merge or Wall, which you turned down on 27 Sep. The status line under the pads stays as it is.

Problem 3

Adjusting the scene often shows no change

S1Recommended

Spotlight the pair

While a pad is hovered or dragged, the other two strains dim in the scene, so the pair you're changing stands out in the real dish. Letting go fades them back.

A view of the controller only, not a setting, so it never reaches the pop-out output or a TV. Question 3 below covers playing straight from the controller.

S2Recommended

Mark the flat ends

Hatch the parts of each Smell pad where the picture doesn't change (below 0 and above +1), and dim the axis word on that side. You see where dragging pays off before you drag.

Fixes findings 1 and 2 without moving anything. The band comes from the measurements and is fixed in code.

S3Recommended

Touch: say when a pair rarely meets

A Touch pad whose two strains share almost no ground gets a "rarely meet" tag, and the hint says Eat has little to bite until Smell brings them together.

Fixes finding 4. Uses the overlap meter's own number.

S4Your call

Squeeze the flat ends

Give 0 to +1 most of the pad's travel and squeeze the flat ends into the rest, so every drag counts. Zero moves left of centre, and the pad loses its symmetric look.

Replaces the flat-around-zero curve from 28 Sep on Smell pads. Touch keeps it.

S5Not proposing

Make avoid strength visible

I tried two quick sim changes on the pad culture: heed the other trail only some of the time, and smell it from further away the stronger the value. Neither made avoid strength show up, so I'm not proposing a sim change now.

The scripts are kept with the record if we want to try again.
