    Touch, the new pair table

    What it does
    When a strain steps on a cell, it changes the other strains' trails there. Right of centre adds to that trail (feeds it); left of centre wipes a share of it (eats it). A strain always lays its own trail as today.
    Why it matters
    Today strains only steer. With Touch, a chaser that also eats wears away the road it follows, and a Wall can become a front that moves. Try Hunt, Gardens and War.
    Interface cost
    Every pair direction holds two numbers, so every editor needs the Smell / Touch switch, and Random has to know which table it rolls.
    In the app
    Feeding is the deposit writing a row instead of one channel. Eating can't be a negative deposit into the 8-bit trail, so the likely route is counting each strain's steps in a second target of the same deposit draw and eroding in the diffuse pass. The deposit is already the most expensive pass (record, Measurements).

    Touch as one knob (Bite)

    What it does
    Touch is copied from Smell instead of edited: each strain eats the trails it avoids, in proportion to how much it avoids them. Bite scales all of it.
    Good
    One slider instead of a second table, so the Affinity interface stays one table. Rivals with Bite gives the War look.
    Can't do
    Eat a trail it follows (Hunt) or feed a trail (Gardens). Those need the full table.
    In the app
    One global setting; the scene works out each pair's eating from att every frame. Same GPU work as the full table.

    Per-strain knobs

    Sensor angle
    Fixed per strain in the app's STRAINS. Together with Turn angle it decides the pattern a strain grows. Already sent to the shader, only not a setting.
    Trail life
    The app fades all four trails at one Trail decay. Per strain, one can leave long highways while another's trail flickers out.
    Share
    The app splits the agents evenly and only the Pipette moves them. A share per strain would let one strain be rare.
    Interface cost
    Rows on the strain card. They don't touch Affinity.

    Pairs

    You do
    Drag one dot per pair. Across is what the first strain does about the second; up is the reverse.
    Good
    Both directions in one drag, the pad names the result, and each pad is a live two-strain culture of just that pair. The Touch switch reuses the same pads.
    Costs
    Tall on a phone. Own trails need their own strip.

    Random and mixes

    Random
    Rolls the table on screen. Smell: every cross relation over the full range, own trails between +0.20 and +1.40 unless Keep own trails is on. Touch: about a third of relations stay at Spares.
    Nudge
    Moves every value in the table on screen a little.
    Mixes
    Values glide to the new table. Every mix keeps both tables and a snapshot taken a few seconds in.
    Auto
    Re-rolls every 8 bars here. In the app, a Receives patch on Random.
