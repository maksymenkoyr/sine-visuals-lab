Affinity Studio

Three ways to set which trails each strain follows or flees. All three edit the same table, and the culture on the left runs on it live. Try Random a few times, then tap an older mix in the strip to go back to it.

    Pairs

    You do
    Drag one dot per pair. Across is how much the first strain follows the second; up is the reverse.
    Good
    Both directions in one drag. The pad names what you will see (Merge, Wall, Chase), and each pad is a live two-strain culture of just that pair.
    Costs
    Tall on a phone. Own trails need their own strip of faders.
    In the app
    A new widget. One drag writes att<a><b> and att<b><a> together.

    Orbit

    You do
    Pull a trail closer to follow it, push it out to flee it. The dashed planet is the strain's own trail.
    Good
    One strain's whole attitude at a glance, and throwing planets around is fun. The arrow on each planet shows how that strain feels back.
    Costs
    One direction at a time. Exact values are harder to hit.
    In the app
    Replaces the relationship web. The centre follows the selected specimen box.

    Rows

    You do
    One continuous slider per trail, no snapping. The words under the track are landmarks only.
    Good
    Exact, predictable, works from the keyboard. Closest to what the panel already does.
    Costs
    Reads like a form. To see a pair you hop between strains.
    In the app
    relationRows.ts with sliders in place of the word chips.

    Random and mixes

    Random
    Rolls every cross relation over the full range. Own trails land between +0.20 and +1.40 so each strain still builds a network, or stay put with Keep own trails.
    Nudge
    Moves every value a little from where it is, to explore around a look you like.
    Mixes
    Values glide to the new table. Every mix lands in the strip with a snapshot taken a few seconds in.
    Auto
    Re-mixes every 8 bars here. In the app it would be a Receives patch on Random, so any trigger source can fire it.
