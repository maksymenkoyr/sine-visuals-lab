Strain Console

Every per-strain setting for all four strains at once, in two layouts to compare, plus what the scene doesn't have yet: Sensor angle, Trail life, and a headcount that follows the settings instead of being set by hand. The culture on the left runs on these values, and a steady 124 BPM pulse stands in for the music on Excitability.

Headcount NEW
How the agents are split between the strains right now. Nobody sets it: agents switch to whichever strain's ink outweighs their own, so a strain with more Nutrient or longer Trail life grows. Rebalance puts it back to 25% each.
Switching NEW
How readily an agent joins the strain whose ink outweighs its own where it stands. At 0 the headcount stays where it is.

Lanes

You do
One row per setting with a thin lane per strain on the same scale. Drag a lane; Link moves all four together.
Good
You see at a glance where each strain sits on a setting and edit any strain without selecting it first. Exact, keyboard friendly.
Costs
Tall: one row per setting.

Knobs

You do
A small knob per strain per setting; drag up or down.
Good
Compact, reads like a mixer, a strain's whole character is one column.
Costs
Knobs are harder to set exactly and to compare than lanes.

In the app

Settings
Each lane or knob is the existing per-strain setting (nutrient, excite, sensor, turn, stride, stain), so pressing one pins that strain's setting and opens its Receives patch as today. Sensor angle and Trail life would be new per-strain settings.
Headcount
Replaces the hand-set Share bar: the split is a result of the other settings. In the scene, the sim pass would rewrite an agent's strain when another strain's ink per agent outweighs its own, a floor keeps every strain alive, and the counts come from a readback instead of being tracked in JS. The pipette and Rebalance keep working on top.
Replaces
The one-strain-at-a-time rows and the tap / checkbox selection. The live previews stay, smaller.
