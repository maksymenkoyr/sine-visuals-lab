How this maps to the app

architecture

The scene declares items and a panel row list as data. It never builds UI itself, which keeps the TV and the private scenes free of panel code.

  • One card, same rows. renderSceneSettings walks the panel list in order: a group heading whenever group changes, then either the named custom widget or an ordinary createControlRow slider for everything else — the Strains group's own widgets are just two more entries in that list, not a separate card.
  • Nothing downstream changes. Every strain control and every global control becomes an ordinary keyed setting, so Receives patches, Auto, saved Looks and share links work on day one.
  • Widgets are reusable. They live in one registry in the panel code. affinityWeb could edit Gates' object pairs, strainBoxes' seedPad half could place Petri drops or Powder emitters.
