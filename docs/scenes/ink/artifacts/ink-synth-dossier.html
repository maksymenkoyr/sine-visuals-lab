What the video is, and how to read a bundle

The author's own description: "a video synthesis program written in Swift and Metal. My program generates a random tree of functions from (x,y) to (r,g,b), and emits shader code for the GPU. Movement comes from interpolating between several vectors of parameters." No fluid simulation, no audio. Every transition in it is a parameter vector passing through a node, and the picture sweeps between a fine ruled-line regime and a broad marbled-ribbon regime over the 200 s loop.

A bundle is one scanned window of the video, and its files are meant to be read in this order:

  1. report.md — Findings first (every sync rule the numbers support), then "Picture, measured" per visual regime, then the bursts.
  2. slitscan.png — the whole window on one time axis, one column per source frame: every cut, flash and pulse at frame resolution.
  3. look.png — per regime: the full-resolution frame, a ×3 centre crop, and the detection overlay. Where the scene's shapes, sizes and stroke come from.
  4. keyframes.png — the look across the window: one tile per regime, before/after of each transition, phrase starts.
  5. timeline.png — audio lanes over visual lanes, beat lines by rank, markers for transitions and regime changes.
  6. bursts/<t>/ — three-second windows at every source frame around each transition: timing.png (when), detail.png (what), motion.png (which way). Drill-down only.

Light-ground caveat. The picture tool's object detector looks for lit shapes on black. On this dark-on-white video it found nothing in most regimes, so the shape numbers below come from a scan-line measurement of full-resolution frames instead: dark-pixel fraction per radius band, ink on the axes against the diagonals, dark-run lengths along rows, and HSV saturation. Radius r is in half-heights of the frame; r = 1 is the top edge.

The open gap. The numbers match, the look only partly does. The reference's ribbons are smooth liquid shapes with organic topology; ours are still families of parallel contour lines, because every stroke is a level set of one function. Closing that means a different stroke construction (thresholded multi-octave noise ribbons), a rebuild of the stroke layer rather than a tune.
