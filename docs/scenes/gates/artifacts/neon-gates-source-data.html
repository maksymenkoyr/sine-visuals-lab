How each regime was measured

lit objectsthreshold the frame at a lit floor, take connected components, classify each by elongation, hollowness and vertex count into bar, blob, disc, panel, ring, hex ring.look.json → objects[]
size vs radiusfit size ∝ rb on the gate-class objects; b near 1 means a perspective tunnel, near 0 a flat pattern.sizeFit
rings, fold, axeshistogram of object radii; rotational fold by best self-overlap; share of objects sitting on a mirror axis or diagonal.rings · fold · onAxis
stroke and glowoutline width from the hollow objects, inside and outside r 0.45; brightness profile stepping outward from a core, fitted to an exponential.stroke · glow.profile · efoldPx
hues and groundhue histogram of lit pixels named by 30° bins; the ground is the median of unlit pixels, with edge/centre brightness.hues · ground
flow and spintrack objects between consecutive frames; radial speed per radius band, share moving outward, rotation in °/s.flow

What was not used

  • The audio. audio.wav is silence; the per-beat reaction tables and the onset columns in the transition table are all zero and were ignored.
  • The beat-rank contact sheets (sheets/rank*.png): they group frames by placeholder beat rank, which means nothing without audio.
  • Ring radii. The measured rings (objects clustering at r ≈ 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 1.0) are not reproduced; objects are placed in one radius band, RADIUS_MIN to RADIUS_MAX, and perspective does the rest.
  • Size envelopes per band and the per-object list (188–200 boxes per regime) were read but not turned into placement rules.
  • Object count as such. The scene caps at 48 objects before mirror copies, so it draws roughly 60–180 items on screen where the reference shows 120–200 detections; this is the biggest remaining visual gap.
Values marked "chosen" in the tables above (accent colours, accent shares, size, rodLen, the ground scale ×0.6, gold's vignette) came from six screenshot rounds against the keyframes, not from a measured line.
