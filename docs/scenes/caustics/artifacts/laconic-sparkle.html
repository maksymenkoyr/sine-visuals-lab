Tighten sits in the Fog family because it moves the same ridge sharpness Fog pulse does. Fog pulse softens the lines on the beat, and Tighten thins them on a hat. Flare sits under Beat flash: both brighten, but Flare lifts only the brightest crests, on treble. Both start at 0, so the default look stays as it is.

The section is plain rows. The custom Sparkle widget and its preview go, since with no shape left to choose there is no curve to preview.

How the scene picks the shape

  1. Every hit starts as a Pluck: it lands at once, then fades.
  2. The length follows the gap between hats: about 2.5 gaps, kept between 0.2 s and 0.6 s (today's default). Busy hats then stay separate flashes and don't smear into a steady glow.
  3. A hit that rings on trembles like a Bell. If the treble is still up 0.12 s after the hit, which happens with an open hat, a ride or a crash, the hit gets Bell's fast vibration. It trembles while the cymbal rings, and stops when the ring dies or the next hit lands.
  4. Swell and Bounce are never picked. Swell grows after the hit, so its peak lands late: the scene can't hear what's coming. Bounce repeats each hit like an echo, the look #508 already turned down.

The music here is made up: hats on a 124 BPM grid, with a treble level that dies fast after a closed hat and rings on after a ride or crash. The curves use the scene's own Pluck and Bell maths. Still to measure on real songs before building: whether the treble level alone tells a ringing hit from a closed hat played over a ride.

Your calls

#713 already holds the rename, the three removals, and Flare and Tighten as sliders. These are new:

  1. The scene picks the shape by the rule above: Pluck, a length that follows the hats, and a Bell tremble on ringing hits. Swell and Bounce go.
  2. Brightness, Density, Spread and Sustain lose their rows and follow Sparkle. They already move with it today, so the default look doesn't change.
  3. Distortion goes. It is 0 by default and draws nothing until it's touched.
  4. The section becomes plain rows: the custom widget and its preview go.
