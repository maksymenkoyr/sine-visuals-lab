Six more effects, none of them from round one

Round one changed order, spin, direction, cracks, depth and the drift's rhythm. These six change distance, local colour, a path, where the water is, tone and which way time runs. They all run live on the same music, over the scene's default look. Pick one to see it big; hold Space to compare it with today.

Every idea is one new row with its own jack, so its source can be rewired like any other setting. None of them adds another pass of the ridges, which is most of the scene's frame.

Also considered

  • Low sunBreakdowns lower the sun and stretch the light into long bands. Round one's Gust already streaks it.
  • Second sunA second light comes in on the drop and every line doubles into a crossing pair. It needs a second pass of the ridges, which would roughly double the frame.
  • Fish shadowsDark shapes swim across the floor. They are shapes on top of the picture, which you turned down for Sparkle; Wake is the same idea done through the water.
  • FreezeA stand-out hit freezes the pool to frost. Round one's Shatter and Stop-motion already cover the hard stop.

My pick

Contour, with Dye as the runner-up.

Contour turns the soft light into something no other scene draws: flat terraces with a bright line on each step. It is the bell shape you asked for on Sparkle, a hard snap that melts away, it transforms the picture rather than adding to it, and it is the cheapest idea here. Dye is the most new to look at: it is the only idea that puts colour in one place. In the app its drops would take the palette's accent colours, not these fixed three.

The pools here run a copy of the scene's shader at its defaults (Neon, Shades + accent), with Beat ripple, Bass swell, Fog pulse, Beat flash, Level glow and Grain sparkle approximated in this page. Wake and Tide unfold over bars; give them a few seconds. A song file is analysed here in three bands; the app's own hit detection will differ.
