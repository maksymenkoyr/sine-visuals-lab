How Beat Ripple listens

In the Caustics scene, a ring spreads across the water when the music does something that stands out. Small sounds that keep repeating don't make rings. This page shows how it decides.

How to read the graph

  • White line: what the ripple hears. Each sound makes it jump up, then it falls back down.

  • Dotted line: how high the next jump has to reach to make a ring. It moves up when there are lots of small sounds, and down after a quiet part.

  • Filled dot: a ring was sent. A bigger dot means a stronger ring.

  • Empty circle: a jump that didn't reach the dotted line, so no ring. (This one is only on this page. The app shows the filled dots.)

What happens, step by step

  1. Hear the sounds

    The app listens for sudden jumps in the music, like a kick drum or a hi-hat. Each one makes the white line jump.

  2. Learn what's normal

    It remembers how big the everyday jumps are, like a steady row of hi-hats. That memory sets the dotted line just above them.

  3. Ring for what stands out

    A jump that goes past the dotted line sends a ring across the water. The further past the line, the stronger the ring.

  4. Forget slowly

    After a few quiet seconds the memory fades and the dotted line comes down, so even a soft sound can make a ring again.

Questions

Why doesn't every sound make a ring?

If every hi-hat sent a ring, new rings would start from the middle all the time and cover up the one from the kick drum. The water would look busy but you couldn't see the beat.

Won't a steady kick drum stop counting as special?

No. The dotted line only learns from the smaller jumps. A kick that's as big as the biggest recent jumps keeps making full rings, however long it plays. Try "Clean beat".

What if I plug in something smooth, like loudness?

It works the same way. It looks at each rise from the last low point up to the top. A big swell makes one ring, a small wobble makes none. Try "Smooth swells".

When hits come very fast, why are the rings lighter?

A hit that lands before the last one has faded can only rise a little way, so its ring is smaller. Fast parts look like quick, light ripples instead of a pile of full rings.

Can I switch it off?

Yes. Open Beat ripple in the panel and press Unplug. With nothing plugged in there are no rings. "Reset to scene default" brings it back to listening for bass and beat hits.

Settings that change the rings

These four change how the rings look, not when they happen.

Ring threshold is the one setting that changes when rings happen: it moves the dotted line. Its technical name is an adaptive threshold. The app estimates the level of the everyday sounds (the noise floor), and the line sits a margin above it.

  • Beat rippleHow strongly the rings bend the light. At 0 the water stays still.
  • Ring widthThin, sharp rings or wide, soft ones.
  • Wave speedHow fast rings travel to the edge.
  • Wave fadeHow quickly rings fade as they travel.
