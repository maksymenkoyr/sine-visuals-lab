What the original actually does, seen between the frames

The first scan ran before the temporal tools existed, so it measured one frame in two and never looked at motion directly. This rescan decodes every frame and adds a slit-scan, six full-rate bursts and motion projections. The reading below replaces the one Tessera was built on.

The lattice stands stillAveraged over a second, the spokes stay sharp. Boxes do not travel inward. My scene's whole premise, tiles flowing to the centre, is wrong.
Box lengths change every frameFrame-difference colour sits only at the box ends. The sides stay grey. The boxes pump in length, most likely from the spectrum.
It is 3D, on a domeEarly on the boxes stand on a ball, in concentric rings like a sea urchin. Later the camera sits inside the field looking down the axis.
The "cuts" are a hue sweepThe tool counts 162 hard cuts, densest late in the clip. They are a full trip round the colour wheel in about two and a half seconds, plus the per-frame length jitter.

What this means for the rebuild

  • A fixed 3D lattice of hollow boxes, laid out in rings on a sphere. No advected sprites, no feedback trails.
  • Box length is the audio reaction: each box follows a band of the spectrum, updated every frame. That matches "continuous activity, no global flash" in the findings.
  • The camera does the regimes: pulled back, the ball reads as the dome; pushed inside and looking down the axis, it reads as the starburst. Steady roll throughout.
  • Hue is a clock that speeds up: slow drift early, a full sweep every few seconds late. Per-face iridescent shading, lit rims.
